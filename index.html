Pro Tips for WebSim Prompting

Be Specific

The more details you provide in your prompt, the better the results will be. Specify colors, styles, functionality, and purpose.

Use References

Upload reference images or link to examples to help guide the AI in creating what you want.

Iterate

Don't expect perfection on the first try. Use the results to refine your prompt and try again.
